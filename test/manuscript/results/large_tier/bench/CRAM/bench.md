# Performance & scalability report

Generated by `pipeline/bench` (`cmd/parity-bench`). Each cell times OUR binary
against the vendored UPSTREAM binary over N repetitions. Two summaries are
reported: the legacy point estimate (wall/CPU = minimum over reps, RSS = peak)
and — the headline for the manuscript — the robust distribution
(median ± IQR per side, the median ours/upstream ratio, and a 95% percentile
bootstrap CI on the wall-time ratio). `ratio = ours / upstream` (< 1.0 = we use
less). The raw per-rep samples for every cell are in the companion `bench.json`.
Slow cells (ratio > 1) are reported plainly, not hidden.
